Stop-by-Stop Notes

1. Place d'Armes Metro to Notre-Dame

  • Elevator access at St. Urbain exit
  • Gentle incline
  • Paved surfaces

2. Notre-Dame Basilica Plaza

  • Wide open plaza
  • Large flat stones (mostly smooth)
  • Benches available
  • Accessible entrance on left

3. Rue Saint-Paul Caution

  • Heavy cobblestones
  • High curb cuts
  • Narrow sidewalks
