The Bells of Notre-Dame

1:12 3:24

notes Transcript

...weighing over 10,000 kilograms, 'Le Gros Bourdon' as it's known locally, was cast in London and brought across the Atlantic in 1841.

Imagine the sheer logistical effort required to haul such a massive object from the port up to these towers without modern machinery.

The sound you hear now is a recording from a special ringing ceremony held during the city's 375th anniversary...

lightbulb Key Facts

  • Bell Name: Jean-Baptiste (Le Gros Bourdon)
  • Weight: 10,900 kg (24,000 lbs)
  • Cast: 1841 by Thomas Mears in London
