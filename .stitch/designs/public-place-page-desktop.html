Place d'Armes

The historic heart of Old Montreal, where centuries of history meet architectural grandeur.

info Why it matters

The birthplace of Montreal and its second-oldest public space. It represents a vital crossroads where the city's indigenous, colonial, religious, and financial histories converge into a single, breathtaking architectural panorama.

history_edu Historical Story

From its origins as a parade ground in 1693, Place d'Armes has continuously evolved. It transformed from a rugged frontier gathering space to the prestigious site of the Maisonneuve Monument, erected in 1895 to commemorate the founder of Montreal.

Later, it became the epicenter of Canada's early financial power, bordered by the magnificent 19th-century 'Golden Mile' skyscrapers that symbolized the nation's burgeoning economic might.

visibility What to notice

  • check_circle The 1895 Maisonneuve Monument by sculptor Louis-Philippe Hébert, featuring intricate bronzework of historical figures.
  • check_circle The striking juxtaposition of architectural styles: the Gothic Revival splendor of Notre-Dame Basilica against the imposing Art Deco lines of the Aldred Building.
  • check_circle The delicate, intricate stonework and classical facade of the Bank of Montreal, Canada's oldest bank building.
