The historic heart of Old Montreal, where centuries of history meet architectural grandeur.

Best Time Morning

Metro Place-d'Armes

Why it matters

As the virtual birthplace of Montreal's civic life, this square holds 17th-century origins. It serves as a visual timeline, showcasing the city's evolution through its drastically varied architectural boundaries.

Historical Story

Originally serving as a military parade ground and public market, Place d'Armes transformed during the 19th and 20th centuries into Canada's primary financial hub, often referred to as the "Wall Street of Canada."
