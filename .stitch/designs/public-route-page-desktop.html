Old Montreal Monuments Loop

A comprehensive walking journey through the historic heart of Montreal, tracing centuries of architecture, culture, and the foundational stories of the city along the St. Lawrence River.

Why this route?

Old Montreal is more than just a picturesque backdrop; it is a living museum of North American history. This carefully curated loop takes you beyond the typical tourist paths, offering a structured journey through the architectural evolution of the city. From the imposing Gothic Revival spires of Notre-Dame to the modern archaeological insights at Pointe-à-Callière, every stop has been selected to provide a cohesive narrative of Montreal's past.

The route is designed for leisurely exploration. The relatively flat terrain and compact geography make it accessible, while the density of landmarks ensures there is always something demanding your attention. We recommend starting early in the morning when the light hits the stone facades perfectly and the narrow streets are quietest.

Route Stops

1

Place d'Armes

The historic square surrounded by distinct architectural eras, anchored by the Maisonneuve Monument.

2

Notre-Dame Basilica

A masterpiece of Gothic Revival architecture. The dramatic interior woodwork and stained glass are unparalleled in the city.

3

Pointe-à-Callière Museum

The birthplace of Montreal. This modern archaeological museum sits exactly where the city was founded in 1642.
