# Walking App Product Context

## Product

Walking App is a Montreal-focused route planning and walk companion app. It helps people choose a walk by goal, compare route options, save routes, follow a live walking session, and understand completion results.

## Register

product

## Users

- Montreal walkers who want a route that fits a step, time, or discovery goal.
- Mobile-first users planning and following a walk outdoors.
- Users who need confidence that route length, save state, location, and completion metrics are accurate.

## Product Purpose

Make walking in Montreal feel easy to plan, compare, follow, and remember. The product should reduce uncertainty: where the walk starts, how long it takes, why one route is recommended, whether a route saved, and what actually happened during the walk.

## UX Priorities

- Keep the app Montreal-only unless a task explicitly expands geography.
- Treat route metric trust as a core design requirement.
- Make route comparison labels match actual route data.
- Make save state visible and hard to misread.
- Put active walk progress ahead of planning-style cards during a live walk.
- Respect mobile safe areas and gesture handles.
- Prefer clear, dense, task-focused mobile UI over marketing-style layouts.

## Tone

Clear, local, calm, and concrete. Avoid generic fitness hype and decorative travel copy. Use route-specific language that helps the user decide or act.

## Anti-References

- Generic AI dashboard aesthetics.
- Decorative cards, glows, gradients, and oversized hero treatments.
- Route labels that sound good but do not match metrics.
- Completion screens that mix planned estimates with actual session results.
