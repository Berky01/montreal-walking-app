# Product Brief — Meaningful Routes

## Working name

**Meaningful Routes**

Other possible names:

- Routeful
- StoryRoute
- Waypoints
- Nice Routes
- WanderPath
- PlacePaths
- Routeworthy
- Walk the Story

## One-sentence pitch

**Discover and follow beautiful, interesting, and meaningful routes around you or wherever you travel.**

## Longer pitch

Meaningful Routes helps people discover cities, neighborhoods, countryside, vacation destinations, and long-distance paths through routes. It can suggest short walks, scenic loops, cultural routes, historical itineraries, architectural walks, food/café routes, nature paths, hidden-gem discoveries, weekend routes, road-trip stops, and pilgrimage-style stages.

The app is flexible: users can start from where they are, search a destination, or plan ahead. It adapts to time, distance, interests, mood, pace, and route mode without forcing a rigid planning flow.

## Core promise

The app helps users answer:

- What can I discover near me right now?
- What is a nice walk I can do in 30–60 minutes?
- What are the most interesting routes in this destination?
- What route fits my mood, available time, and interests?
- What places are worth walking for?
- Can I follow the route live and remember what I completed?

## Product category

A **place and route discovery app**.

It sits between:

- Google Maps
- Strava
- AllTrails
- Komoot
- GPSmyCity
- VoiceMap
- Atlas Obscura
- Wanderlog
- Roadtrippers
- UNESCO/World Heritage apps
- Camino/pilgrimage apps

## What it is not

- Not a generic walking tracker
- Not a fitness-first app
- Not a Google Maps clone
- Not only a UNESCO directory
- Not only a planner
- Not only a travel blog
- Not a social network
- Not a dating app

## Core modes

1. **Around Me** — spontaneous nearby discovery.
2. **Explore a Destination** — search a city/neighborhood and discover routes there.
3. **Build / Filter a Route** — flexible filtering by time, distance, mood, interests, and mode.
4. **Follow Route** — active walk/navigation companion.
5. **Remember** — saved routes, completed history, favorite places.
6. **Future Travel Modes** — weekend, road trip, heritage/UNESCO, pilgrimage.

## MVP positioning

**A Montreal route discovery app that helps users find, compare, follow, save, and remember meaningful walking routes.**

Montreal is the MVP city only. The architecture must support global city expansion.
