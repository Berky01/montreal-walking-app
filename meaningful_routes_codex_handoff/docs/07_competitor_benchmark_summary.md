# Competitor Benchmark Summary

## Competitive categories

| Category | Competitors | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday maps | Google Maps, Apple Maps | Fast nearby discovery, but not meaningful route-first |
| Fitness/outdoor | Strava, AllTrails, Komoot, Wikiloc, Outdooractive, Footpath | Great routing/tracking, weak on city culture/story |
| City walking tours | GPSmyCity, VoiceMap, izi.TRAVEL, Rick Steves | Strong self-guided tours, often fixed/static |
| Travel planners | Wanderlog, Tripomatic/Sygic, Roadtrippers, Polarsteps | Good planning/memory, less spontaneous discovery |
| Hidden/cultural places | Atlas Obscura, Culture Trip, Mapstr | Great place discovery, weak route following |
| Heritage/UNESCO | UNESCO, World Heritage App | Authoritative data, not practical route companion |
| Camino/pilgrimage | Gronze, Wise Pilgrim, Buen Camino, Compostelle, Pilgrim.es | Strong long-route logistics, narrow scope |
| Tour marketplaces | GetYourGuide, Tripadvisor, GuruWalk, FREETOUR | Booking monetization, not self-guided flexible routes |

## Open market gap

No dominant product owns:

> Spontaneous meaningful route discovery + flexible route filtering + active walking companion + travel/heritage context + memory/history.

## What to copy

- Google Maps: fast map/search/bottom sheet interactions
- AllTrails: route cards, difficulty, map preview, offline confidence
- Strava: active session and completion recap
- Komoot: route confidence and navigation clarity
- GPSmyCity: city walking route library
- VoiceMap/izi.TRAVEL: story layer and GPS-triggered content later
- Atlas Obscura: curiosity, hidden-gem framing
- Wanderlog/Roadtrippers: trip/day/stop organization later
- World Heritage App: visited/favorites/stats for heritage layer later
- Camino apps: stages, elevation, services, long-route logistics later

## What not to become

- Google Maps clone
- Strava for casual walks
- AllTrails for cities
- GPSmyCity with prettier UI
- Atlas Obscura with route lines
- Wanderlog but smaller
- UNESCO checklist app

## Winning angle

**The route is the product, not the pin.**

The app wins if it suggests routes users would not easily discover or assemble themselves.
