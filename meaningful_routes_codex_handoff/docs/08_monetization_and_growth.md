# Monetization and Growth

## Early monetization options

### 1. Premium city packs

Free:

- basic routes
- limited saved routes
- live walk mode

Paid:

- premium curated routes
- offline route cards
- hidden gems
- themed packs
- city guide export

Example products:

- Montreal Premium Walks — $6.99 to $9.99 one-time
- Montreal Discovery Pass — $19.99/year
- All Cities Pass — later

### 2. Hotel / tourism B2B

Hotels, hostels, student residences, events, and tourism boards can provide branded walking routes from their location.

Examples:

- “90-minute Old Montreal walk from this hotel”
- “Rainy-day route near the convention center”
- “Family-friendly route from the lobby”

### 3. Sponsored local stops

Cafés, museums, galleries, and local shops can sponsor clearly labeled route stops. Must preserve trust.

### 4. Guided tours / tickets affiliates

Later, route details can include:

- museum tickets
- walking tours
- local guide tours
- attractions

### 5. Road-trip monetization later

When road-trip mode exists:

- hotels
- car rentals
- tours/tickets
- paid PDF itinerary

## Growth loops

- Share completed route card
- Share route link
- Web SEO route pages
- Hotel QR codes
- “Send to phone” from web
- City pack recommendations
- Collections: “my Montreal weekend ideas”

## SEO web layer

Potential pages:

- Best walks in Montreal
- Old Montreal walking route
- Architecture walk Montreal
- Romantic walks Montreal
- Rainy day walks Montreal
- 30-minute walks near Old Montreal
- Hidden gems walk Montreal
- Later: UNESCO sites near Rome, 7-day Italy heritage route

## Avoid early

- Ads inside active walk
- Aggressive subscriptions before value is proven
- Over-sponsored routes
- Paywalling basic safety/navigation
- Boosted route placements that hurt quality
